My Presets for Liminal Amp.

Each preset is a small text file here, <name>.na2p: the settings, the amp (capture) and the cab (IR) you saved.
Save, load and delete them from the plugin's MY PRESETS list; name, edit, rename and delete them on your phone
(NAME ON PHONE). You can also rename or delete the files from a computer: press RESCAN and the list follows.
This folder is kept when you update the plugin.
