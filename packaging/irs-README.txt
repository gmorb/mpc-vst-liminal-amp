Put cabinet impulse responses (.wav files) here, or in any folder named "irs" on your memory card or the Force's
internal drive. Subfolders become categories. After adding files, press RESCAN.

IRs must be MONO (as in the official NAM plugin), 16-, 24- or 32-bit PCM or 32-bit float, at any sample rate.
A stereo IR shows "IR must be mono": export the left channel or a mono mix.
IRs longer than 8192 samples are cut to 8192 (as the official NAM plugin does); IR LENGTH "Short" cuts them to
4096 for lower CPU.
