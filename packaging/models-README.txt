Put Neural Amp Modeler captures (.nam files) here, or in any folder named "models" on your memory card or the
Force's internal drive. Subfolders become categories (e.g. models/Fender/Twin.nam shows as "Twin", "Fender").

A2 captures run at their Lite size (A2-Full is too heavy for this hardware). Other NAM files (standard WaveNet,
LSTM) also load, but may use much more CPU: try one at a time. After adding files, press RESCAN.
